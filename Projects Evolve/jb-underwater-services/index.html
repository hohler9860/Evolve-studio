Who We Are

Built on a Passion for Diving

J&B Underwater Services was established in 2013 by business partners Brian LeBlanc of Roslindale, MA and John Lyons of Dorchester, MA.

Recognizing a market opportunity in marine services, they leveraged their passion for SCUBA diving to create a company that provides professional underwater services to boat owners throughout Massachusetts and beyond.

From the sport of SCUBA to professional underwater marine services, J&B brings decades of combined experience, certified expertise, and a commitment to getting every job done right.

What We Do

Our Services

Professional underwater services backed by decades of diving experience and certified expertise.

Hull Cleaning

Your boat hull is always exposed to harsh elements. Marine growth, corrosion, and barnacle buildup require professional removal. We are professional divers who know how to clean boat hulls correctly.

Zinc Replacement

Protect your vessel from galvanic corrosion with our professional zinc anode replacement service. We inspect, remove, and replace sacrificial anodes to extend the life of your boat's underwater metals.

SCUBA Instruction

SCUBA diving uses a self-contained underwater breathing apparatus to explore beneath the surface. We teach and guide divers of all experience levels to master underwater diving.

Mooring Work

Unlike ordinary anchoring, a mooring is a permanent solution designed for boats that will remain unattended for extended periods. We handle professional mooring installation and maintenance.

Boston Scuba Academy

Expert Diving Instruction Since 1980+

Providing expert diving instruction in Massachusetts for over 40 years. We welcome divers of all experience levels to explore Massachusetts waters and beyond.

Basic Open Water

Six-lesson basic course with two days of certification dives. Classes offered every month upon request. Universal Referral Forms available.

Advanced Open Water

Deep and night diving, search and recovery, underwater navigation. Includes the Chester Poling shipwreck dive—New England's most accessible large wreck.

Guided Dives

Available for both new and experienced divers. Equipment rental available or bring your own gear. Shore dive announcements posted on social media.

Monthly Classes

Open water classes available every month upon request

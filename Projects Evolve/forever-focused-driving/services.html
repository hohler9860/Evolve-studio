$120/hour

Private lessons are completely one-on-one. No other students will be in the vehicle during your scheduled lesson. Only you and your instructor.

This option is especially popular with beginners, nervous drivers, or anyone who prefers a more individualized and private learning environment, including licensed individuals looking for a refresher course.

How Many Lessons Will I Need?

We cannot accurately determine how many lessons a student will need without first assessing their driving skills in person.

On average, a complete beginner may require approximately 15 to 20 hours of professional instruction to progress from the beginner stage to being road-test ready.

However, every student learns at a different pace. The amount of instruction needed may vary depending on prior driving experience, consistency between lessons, how frequently lessons are scheduled, and how well the student retains and applies previously learned skills.

Some students may require fewer hours, while others may benefit from additional instruction.

We offer pickup and drop-off anywhere within Cambridge.

Road Test Sponsorship
Starting at $180

Road test sponsorship is available at the Watertown RMV for $180 and includes:

  • Use of our driving school vehicle for your road test
  • A Forever Focused Driving Academy representative serving as your road test sponsor

Road test sponsorship at other RMV locations may also be available upon request. Because Watertown is the closest road test location to our primary service area, it is offered at our lowest sponsorship rate.

Sponsorship fees for other RMV locations will increase based on location and travel distance. This additional cost accounts for mileage, fuel, travel time, and the extended amount of time our vehicle and instructor are away from our Cambridge service area, during which we would otherwise be conducting scheduled driving lessons.

Please TEXT (617) 852-4179 with your requested RMV location for availability and pricing.

If you would like additional practice before your road test, you may also book a one- or two-hour driving lesson prior to your test, provided availability permits.

Icon 01 — refined.

Five sharpened iterations of the asterisk-star concept. Less basic, more brand. Each shown at hero size, 18px nav size, and 16px favicon size so you can see how it scales.

Current placeholder on the site = 01a "Original". Pick A–E and I lock it in across nav + favicon + meta.
Original 01a · current

The current placeholder. Basic 8-point asterisk. Reference baseline.

Compass Burst 01b · my top pick

Claude-style asymmetric weights — 4 thick cardinal blades + 4 thin diagonals at 40% opacity. Crisp at small sizes, dimensional at large.

Double Star 01c

Two asterisks at different rotations + scales — creates depth and motion. Reads as "evolution / transformation."

Technical 01d

Outlined version with a solid center dot. Technical / engineered feel. Pairs well with the "I build by hand" positioning.

Chiseled 01e

Each blade has a subtle inset cut near the base — gives a chiseled / faceted feel. Hardest to read at 16px, brilliant at 64px+.

Starburst 12 01f

12-point burst with alternating opacity — feels closer to a Manus-style geometric mark. Loses some clarity at favicon size.

My pick: 01b (Compass Burst)

It's the closest to the Claude/Anthropic asymmetric-weight aesthetic without being a copy. Four thick cardinal blades read instantly at every size, the thin diagonals at 40% opacity add detail at hero size that disappears gracefully at favicon size. That's how you know a logo is well-engineered.

Backup: 01d (Technical) — fits "I build by hand" positioning perfectly. The outlined version + solid dot is a power move.

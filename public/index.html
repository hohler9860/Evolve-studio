03 / Process

Three steps. No deck.

I don't do proposals or discovery decks. I build a sample. You decide.

  1. 01

    You tell me about your business.

    15 minutes on Zoom or a quick form. What you do, what you sell, what's broken about your current site (if you have one).

  2. 02

    I build a free mockup. 48 hours.

    Real homepage. Real copy. Real design. Not a slideshow. Not "AI-generated." Hand-built using my actual production stack.

  3. 03

    Love it → we launch. Hate it → walk.

    If you say yes, full site goes live in 1–3 weeks. If you say no, you owe me nothing. That's the deal.

06 / FAQ

Common questions.

How fast can you actually build my site?

Mockup: 48 hours. Full site live: 1–3 weeks depending on tier. I work alone, so there's no agency back-and-forth — you talk directly to the person building it.

What if I don't like the mockup?

You walk. No charge. No hard feelings. The mockup is genuinely free — I'd rather build for someone who's excited than convince someone who's hesitant.

Do you only do small business websites?

Websites are the most common entry point but I build voice agents, content automations, custom tools, membership platforms, e-commerce — anything that needs a brain and a UI. Message me with what you have in mind.

What about hosting and maintenance?

Included in your monthly. Hosting, SSL, updates, small content changes, performance monitoring. Cancel anytime, no contracts.

Do I own the site?

Yes. Code, content, domain — all yours. If you ever want to leave I'll export everything cleanly. No lock-in.

Who's actually doing the work — you, or AI?

Me. I use AI as a tool (same way a designer uses Figma) but every decision, every line of copy, every design choice is mine. No "AI slop" mockups.
